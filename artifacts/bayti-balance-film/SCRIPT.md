# Bayti Balance — film treatment & shot script

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** A family can make wellbeing feel like small, shared rituals rather than another thing to manage.
- **Identity:** Bayti Balance's cream, deep-navy, turquoise, coral, mint and soft-sun palette; expressive editorial display type paired with a clear geometric sans.
- **Hero:** The app's daily rhythm, visualized as a small home-shaped mark that unfolds into Move, Eat and Recover.
- **Motion:** App-interface elements assemble from tactile cut-paper shapes; an animated path carries attention between domains; the finale compresses the whole system into the home mark.
- **Arc:** A human-scale welcome becomes playful movement, practical nourishment, and a quieter wind-down before a calm, graphic resolution.
- **Payoff:** The feature worlds collapse into the Bayti Balance home mark, making the family's day feel like one connected place.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.

## Full script

### Shot 01 / 00.00–04.00s / 4.00s — “A LITTLE BALANCE.”

**Purpose:** Introduce Bayti Balance as approachable family life support, not another clinical tracker.

**Exact content and holds:** “A LITTLE” appears at 00.20s and holds through 03.65s. “BALANCE.” appears at 00.55s and holds through 03.65s. Small label “A FAMILY WELLBEING COMPANION” appears at 01.05s and holds through 03.65s. The board shows “Today, together” and three labels: “Move”, “Eat”, “Recover”.

**Composition:** Warm cream field. A home-shaped badge is large and cropped into the upper-right. Left third owns the type (headline 8–10vmin; eyebrow 2vmin). On the right, a floating daily board is tilted slightly in perspective, with three pastel activity tiles. Deep navy type, small turquoise and coral details, warm directional paper-shadow.

**Artistic idea:** The tiny home icon behaves like a stamp: it lands, and its outline opens into a tangible daily board. A circular turquoise ring orbits the board, giving the day a continuous rhythm.

**Timed choreography:** 00.00–00.20s: navy house stamp punches into frame with a short stiff spring; a thin cream outline is visible immediately. 00.20–00.55s: “A LITTLE” reveals by masked line. 00.55–00.95s: “BALANCE.” assembles as the letters close their tracking. 00.45–01.25s: board unfolds from the house stamp in two hinged panels; its tiles pop in one at a time at 00.70, 00.90 and 01.10s. 01.35–02.60s: turquoise orbit traces around the board, passing behind the title zone; the last tile gently tilts into readable alignment. 02.80–04.00s: camera pushes into the ring; the ring sweeps diagonally across frame and remains in transit at the cut.

**Transition contract:** The moving ring becomes the route line in Shot 02, at the same size and direction. The board folds away as a coral plane turns through camera, combining an object-scale push with a panel rotation.

**Assets and technique:** All original vector/cut-paper UI illustration in SVG/HTML; no external image assets required. CSS perspective, clipping masks, drawn orbit, staggered transforms.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental music opens with a restrained pluck and warm pulse. Cut at 4.00s is on a measured onset in the locally synthesized bed.

### Shot 02 / 04.00–08.00s / 4.00s — “MOVE, YOUR WAY.”

**Purpose:** Show movement as playful, adaptable family time; ground the feature in the existing oasis and activity ideas.

No reuse of Shot 01's text column/card layout: headline sits as a small top-right banner; action owns the full stage.

**Artistic idea:** One continuous path is both a route through the oasis and a progress line that builds the scene. Each step leaves a piece of the oasis in its wake.

**Timed choreography:** 04.00s: ring from previous shot completes a loop and is now the route; coral plane flips away to reveal landscape below. 04.15–04.80s: path draws across the scene in a single sweeping stroke. 04.35–05.10s: paired child/adult silhouettes step in at different depths; each contact triggers a soft ripple and a new oasis piece (leaf, canopy, water tile). 05.25–05.85s: session card folds out from the path and settles at bottom-left; “MOVE, YOUR WAY.” snaps into the clear sky zone. 06.20–06.95s: “INDOOR / OUTDOOR OPTIONS” traces in beside a heat/sun dial that flips from sun to shade. 07.20–08.00s: route curls toward the lens; its loop enlarges to become the meal plate rim. **Transition contract:** Route loop transforms into a clean circular plate rim; the card's coral edge becomes a warm table plane. Combine curl-to-camera with a vertical panel fold.

**Assets and technique:** Original simple illustrative figures and oasis geometry; SVG route and contact ripples; no photographic asset required for this interface/illustration film.

**Sound intent:** Music gains a brushed percussion pulse. Cut at 8.00s is on a measured onset in the locally synthesized bed.

**Assets and technique:** Original top-down plate and paper-shape ingredients, CSS/SVG composition, staged notes, circular masks.

**Sound intent:** Percussion pares back into a warmer sustained note at the quieter recovery shift. Cut at 13.00s is on a measured onset in the locally synthesized bed.

### Shot 04 / 13.00–18.00s / 5.00s — “REST IS PART OF THE PLAN.”

**Purpose:** Close the daily loop with gentle recovery and transparent framing around sleep preferences.

**Composition:** Deep indigo night field, moon dial centered and large, stars held at very low contrast. Type is split left/right around the dial, not layered on top. Three wind-down checks arrange along the lower edge with warm ivory and lavender details.

**Artistic idea:** The plate/moon dial ticks once around to become a sleep measure; each settled breath turns a wind-down checklist mark into a star, showing recovery as a sequence of small choices.

**Timed choreography:** 13.00–13.55s: plate-to-moon aperture opens; inherited star streak is visible inside the aperture immediately. 13.35–14.20s: headline reveals on opposite masks, with “REST” in pale gold and supporting phrase in ivory. 14.00–14.65s: moon dial rotates a quarter turn and stops at 8:15, visually echoing a preference log rather than a medical gauge. 14.60–16.60s: the three routine labels arrive one at a time; each check mark traces into its star while a calm expanding ring breathes once. 16.60–17.45s: footer resolves; orbit lines reduce to the single roofline geometry of the home mark. 17.45–18.00s: roofline expands through the lens, paired with a clean vertical split that flips to warm cream.

**Assets and technique:** Original vector night sky and interface dial; CSS rings and SVG line paths; no medical visuals or health efficacy claims.

**Sound intent:** Briefly reduce rhythmic density while maintaining the music bed; warm resolution begins near 18s. Cut at 18.00s is on a measured onset in the locally synthesized bed.

## Pre-code variety review

Each neighboring shot changes at least two of field, scale, primary composition, dominant motion and visual medium: paper UI assembly → wide route landscape → overhead food still-life → quiet night dial → minimal typographic lockup. The subject always advances: daily rhythm, shared movement, practical kitchen support, wind-down, connected brand. Key object carries are the orbit/ring, route loop/plate, plate/moon, dial/roof and logo/loop stamp. All headline and callout zones are reserved away from their respective hero objects. Every scene includes post-entrance activity and a final 15–20% handoff move.
